Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

BrightPenny ("the app") is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data stored on your device

Transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, categories and settings are stored locally in a database on your phone. The app does not send this data to any server operated by us.

Google Drive (optional)

If you enable Google Drive backup, the app uses Google Sign-In and the Drive API with the scope drive.file. This scope only allows the app to read and write files it created itself in your Drive — never any other file. Backup files are encrypted on your device before upload. You can revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Crypto price lookups

For crypto accounts, the app queries public price APIs (CoinGecko) and public blockchain RPC endpoints to compute balances. Only the wallet addresses you add are sent to those endpoints. No personal identifiers are attached.

Foreign-exchange rates

For multi-currency transactions, the app queries a public FX rate API. Only the currency codes are sent.

Analytics and ads

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be announced in the app.
